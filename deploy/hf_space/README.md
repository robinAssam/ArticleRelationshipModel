# Article Relationship Model

Describe a legal problem in Dutch or English and find the statutory articles
that apply (WGBO and Book 6 of the Dutch Civil Code). Each article is split
into atoms, the smallest pieces that carry a single legal meaning, and
labelled with a 9-label schema; questions are decomposed the same way and
matched label by label, with an auditable trail of why each article came up.

This demo is password protected. Source code:
https://github.com/robinAssam/ArticleRelationshipModel

Bachelor thesis project, Law & Tech Lab, Maastricht University.
